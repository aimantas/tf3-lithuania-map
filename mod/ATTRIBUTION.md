# Data attribution and licences

## OpenStreetMap (towns, town names, populations, river courses)

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The town presets, the town-name list and the reference tables (`map-kit/reference/towns.json`, `towns-osm.json`, `rivers-osm.json`) are derived from or contain OpenStreetMap data and are offered under the ODbL. The heightmaps are produced works that use OpenStreetMap river courses. The source snapshots used are included in `map-kit/reference`.

## Terrain (heightmaps)

Elevation from Mapzen Terrain Tiles via the AWS Open Data Registry (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), terrarium encoding, zoom 9. For this area the tiles combine:

- EU-DEM: produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union, EU-DEM layers.
- SRTM: courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
- ETOPO1: U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

The full upstream attribution text is in `map-kit/reference/terrain-attribution.md`.

## Country outline and lake polygon

Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), public domain.
